About

System Architecture

How the AI Agent processes your farm inputs into crop recommendations.

1

Farmer Input

Location, season, soil NPK, pH, weather parameters, water availability, and land area.

↓
2

Input Validation

All numeric values are range-checked. Missing or out-of-range values return friendly errors.

↓
3

Environmental Analysis

Temperature, humidity, rainfall, pH, and nutrients are evaluated against reference ranges.

↓
4

ML Crop Prediction

A trained Random Forest Classifier (200 estimators) scores all crops using predict_proba().

↓
5

Seasonal & Water Analysis

Seasonal suitability and water compatibility are checked from the crop knowledge base.

↓
6

Decision Layer & Ranking

Combined score = ML 60% + Season 20% + Water 20%. Crops are ranked accordingly.

↓
7

Explainable Recommendation

A plain-language advisory and step-by-step explanation are generated.

Technology Stack

Python 3 Flask Scikit-learn Random Forest Pandas NumPy joblib Chart.js HTML5 / CSS3 / JS

📋 System Disclaimer

This system is an academic AI-assisted decision-support tool developed for crop planning purposes. Model suitability scores reflect the output of a machine learning classifier trained on a crop recommendation dataset. They should not be interpreted as guaranteed probabilities of agricultural success. Seasonal and water compatibility assessments are based on a general knowledge base and may not account for local micro-climatic conditions. Always consult qualified agricultural experts and local extension services before making farming decisions.
